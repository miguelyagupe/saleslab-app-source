# F-04 — Reconciliation Engine

F-04 supplies seven immutable financial rules at version `1`. Tolerance policies
are intentionally caller-supplied, versioned contracts: the catalogue contains no
business threshold, locale-dependent choice, or model-selected default.

`REC-GROWTH-001` is the only basis for an organic-growth claim. An incomplete
bridge is blocked. Likewise, lease additions (including the synthetic JM-FIN-006
535 M€ case) do not establish expansion attribution without the full lease bridge.
Statutory and adjusted EBITDA operands and variants remain separate.

Working-capital reconciliation is deliberately deferred. The integrated domain
does not define a sufficiently precise working-capital movement taxonomy (including
non-cash movements, acquisitions, FX, reclassifications, and disposal effects), so
adding a rule here would invent financial concepts rather than version an existing
contract.
